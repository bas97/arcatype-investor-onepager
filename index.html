01 — Market dynamics

There have never been more brands on the market than there are today.

For brands

Lost in the noise

More brands means more noise. Independent labels with genuine craft and considered values are being outspent on every channel that matters.

The ones making the best products are not always the ones getting found. Arcatype gives them a new retail channel with structured, credible data.

The problem
For consumers

No trusted way to find them

Greenwashing has worn consumers down. Years of vague claims, recycled-plastic capsule collections, and certification badges with no substance behind them have made genuine trust difficult to extend.

The options have multiplied but the systems have not improved. Consumers know what they are looking for — they just have no organised way to find it.

Automation is creating a barbell trend.

Automation

A new discovery channel most brands don't know exists

Answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews are now the first place a growing number of consumers go when they want to know what to buy, who makes it well, and why it is worth the price.

The tools to optimise for this exist, but they were built for scale, not for the independent brand making forty pieces a season in a studio in Ghent. Arcatype structures and surfaces brand intelligence specifically for answer engines, ensuring the brands that deserve to be found in these conversations are the ones that appear in them.

The barbell
Desire for presence

Discovery is moving offline again

Consumers are seeking out physical experiences precisely because the digital world has become too fast, too flat, and too easy to fake.

The brands winning culturally right now are the ones showing up in rooms, not just feeds. Arcatype Editions is built on that shift. The desire for presence is not a retreat from modernity. It is the most modern thing happening in the market right now.
